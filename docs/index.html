Features

A full annotation toolkit, right in the page

Everything here is built into the extension — no sign-up, no add-ons.

Ink that scrolls with content

Strokes are stored in the page's coordinate space, so an arrow next to a paragraph stays there through scrolling, infinite scroll, and SPA navigation — even inside inner scroll areas like chat threads.

Pen, highlighter & shapes

Freehand pen, marker-style highlighter, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse (outline or filled). Hold Shift to snap to a square, circle, or 45° line.

Smart multi-line text

Click to place text, type inline, and it wraps to the window. Five font families, your own line breaks, and double-click to re-edit anything you've written.

Auto-numbered step stamps

Drop markers that auto-increment (1, 2, 3…) — perfect for step-by-step callouts, tutorials, and design feedback.

Select, move & resize

Nothing is locked once it's down. Marquee or click to select, drag to move, use handles to resize, and a stroke-level eraser removes whole marks cleanly.

Mouse, trackpad & touch

Detects how you point and tunes gestures and touch-target sizes to match — two-finger scroll, pinch-zoom pass-through, and palm rejection while a stylus draws.

A toolbar you control

A draggable, collapsible floating bar. Set its size, corner shape, colour, and position, choose which tools appear and in what order, and switch light / dark / auto themes.

Export to PNG

Save your annotated page as an image — the visible area, or a full-page render that captures every stroke across the whole document, even what's off-screen.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no tracking, no network requests. Settings sync through your own Chrome account; drawings stay on your device.

How it works

From install to first stroke in under a minute

1

Install

Add Draw on Web to Chrome and pin it from the puzzle-piece menu for one-click access.

2

Toggle draw mode

Open any page, click the icon or press Ctrl/⌘+Shift+D. A slim floating toolbar appears.

3

Pick a tool & draw

Choose pen, highlighter, a shape, text, or a step stamp, then draw right on the page.

4

Scroll, export, done

Your marks stay anchored as you scroll. Switch to Cursor mode to use the site, or export a PNG.
